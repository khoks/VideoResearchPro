# English words as Hindi captions spell them in Devanagari (T-1.16.2.8).
#
# YouTube's Hindi ASR writes English speech in Devanagari: "मशीन लर्निंग" is
# "machine learning". Script says nothing about those words, so
# `code_mixing_service.tag_tokens` looks them up here.
#
# Written from general knowledge before the labelled set's Devanagari units
# were read, the way hindi_romanised.txt was (D-080). Base forms only: the
# tagger also strips the plural and oblique endings (प्रोडक्ट्स, मशीनों, कंपनियों)
# and folds nasal spellings (कॉन्सेप्ट, कॉंसेप्ट), so one entry covers those.
#
# NOT here, on purpose: English words that are also ordinary Hindi words.
# आई, दे, इन, कम, बस, मन, पर, पास, बार, सन, वन, नाम, सी, ही, हर, लो, सर, मैं,
# मच (मचना), टेक (टेकना), बीन (बीनना, the instrument), पीपल (the tree), होम (ritual fire).
# A wrong "English" costs more than a miss: it hides Hindi from the share that
# decides how a source is quoted.
