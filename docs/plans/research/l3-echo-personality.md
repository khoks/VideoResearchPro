## Summary

- **OpenAI's self-serve fine-tuning platform is being shut down.** The official pricing and SFT guide pages now say "OpenAI is winding down the fine-tuning platform"; the deprecations page gives the staircase: no new orgs since 2026-05-07, inactive orgs cut 2026-07-02, **no new jobs for anyone after 2027-01-06**, and every fine-tunable base model (gpt-4.1/-mini, gpt-4o) retires 2027-01-14 with fine-tunes included. "Mode B on OpenAI" is not a viable plan for a new product.
- The only hosted alternatives are Bedrock (Claude **3 Haiku only**, us-west-2, Provisioned Throughput required; Llama 3.1/3.2/3.3, Nova) and Vertex AI (Gemini 3.5 Flash, 3.1 Flash-Lite, 2.5 Pro/Flash/Flash-Lite; the 2.5 line retires mid/late Oct 2026). Local QLoRA on an 8B model needs 6 GB VRAM as an absolute floor (Unsloth), 12–16 GB in practice; Unsloth core / Axolotl / PEFT are all Apache-2.0.
- The best 2025–26 evidence says **few-shot exemplars (≈5) capture most of the available prompt-time style gain, with little return beyond that**, and **direct LoRA raises style accuracy but collapses quality** (preference rate 97.9% → 38.1% on one benchmark). Retrieval + critique loops are the right v1; fine-tuning is a later, guarded experiment.
- Style signals that are cheap, open and runnable per note: classical stylometry (function words, sentence-length distribution, punctuation, char n-grams; Burrows' Delta via `faststylometry`, MIT), `TextDescriptives` (Apache-2.0, spaCy), and author embeddings `LUAR-MUD` (Apache-2.0, 82.5 M params, 512-d) or `StyleDistance` (MIT, ~0.1 B). **Avoid** LIWC-22 (commercial licence) and Wegmann's `Style-Embedding` (no licence declared).
- **No automatic metric is credible enough on its own to gate the product.** LLM-as-personalized-judge has "low and inconsistent agreement" with humans unless it abstains on uncertain cases (then >80% on binary tasks); style metrics are entangled with content. Gate on a calibrated authorship-verification score **plus** a blinded "which one did you write?" test where the user is the judge.
- Critique-refine loops with a profile-conditioned critic (tone / vocabulary / sentence structure / topicality) show +8.5–12% G-Eval over retrieval-only personalization across 3–5 iterations (PerFine, 2025) — but no paper has run critique vs. more-examples head-to-head with human raters.
- No comparable product publishes a numeric cold-start threshold. NotebookLM, Gemini Personal Intelligence, ChatGPT memory and Grammarly's voice profile all rely on opt-in plus progressive accumulation; Apple's personal-context Siri slipped repeatedly and is still unconfirmed as shipped.
- Regulatory floor as of Oct 2026: EU AI Act Art. 50 applies from **2026-08-02** (marking duty deferred to 2026-12-02 for systems already on the market); the Code of Practice exempts text under ~200 tokens from watermarking and lets free-form text carry a single marking technique; private emails/drafts are outside 50(4) but a bubble that converses with the user is inside 50(1). India's Feb-2026 IT-Rules amendment covers audio/visual SGI only — **text is out of scope**. OpenAI's usage policy bans impersonation of others without consent; a self-voice tool is fine, third-party voices are not.
- Bilingual users genuinely shift per language: Hindi–English Twitter users prefer Hindi for negative opinion and swearing (odds 1.5–6× vs 0.1–0.6× for English; 6.5× in code-switched tweets) and English for positive/professional content; Big-Five self-reports shift with language (cultural frame switching). Voice signals therefore need a **language/script dimension** (en, hi-Deva, Hinglish-Latn, mixed) and all the open style embeddings are English-trained.
- Floating-assistant conventions: Cmd/Ctrl+K is the established palette trigger (Linear, Slack, Superhuman, Vercel, GitHub, Notion search); **Cmd+E is the macOS system-wide "Use Selection for Find"** and clashes in Safari/Chrome — do not use it. WAI-ARIA APG rules for dialogs (focus in/out, Escape, Tab wrap, `aria-modal` only when truly modal) apply directly.

## 1. Prior art and academic work

### Benchmarks and persona papers
- **LaMP — "When Large Language Models Meet Personalization"** (Salemi, Mysore, Bendersky, Zamani; arXiv Apr 2023, ACL 2024). Seven tasks (3 classification, 4 generation) with per-user profiles; proposes retrieval-augmenting the prompt with items from the user's own history (term-matching, semantic, time-aware retrievers); tests zero-shot and fine-tuned models. Code: `lamp-benchmark` GitHub org. https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.11406 · https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.399
- **PersonaLLM** (Jiang et al.; arXiv 2305.02547, Findings NAACL 2024). Assigns Big-Five personas to GPT-3.5/4, checks BFI self-reports (consistent, large effect sizes), analyses writing with LIWC, and finds human raters identify traits up to ~80% — but accuracy "drops significantly when annotators were informed of AI authorship". Relevant as evidence that *told-it's-AI* framing changes human judgement, i.e. blind A/B is mandatory. https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.02547
- **"Catch Me If You Can? Not Yet: LLMs Still Struggle to Imitate the Implicit Writing Styles of Everyday Authors"** (arXiv 2509.14543; Findings EMNLP 2025). The most directly relevant study. Four English corpora, ~400 authors (Enron email 150, Blog 100, CCAT50 news 50, Reddit 100); models GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini, Gemini-2.0-Flash, Gemma-3-27B, DeepSeek-V3, Llama-4-Maverick; default 5 exemplars + content summary. Metrics: authorship attribution (AA) and verification (AV) classifiers (Longformer, ModernBERT), LIWC + WritePrints style vectors scored by Mahalanobis distance, GPTZero, and semantic similarity as a sanity check. Headline numbers: human text validates the pipeline (AV 87.7–91.4%, AA top-5 79.8–95.5%); GPT-4o 5-shot AA top-5 = 86.64% (news), 59.65% (email), 39.39% (blog), 27.12% (Reddit); AV ~95–97% for news/email but ~17–21% for blogs. Few-shot beats zero-shot everywhere; **2→10 exemplars yields little further gain**; content-matched exemplar selection *hurt* attribution; length-matching gave small AA gains but weaker style fidelity. **No human evaluation**; informal registers remain hard. https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14543v1 · https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.532.pdf
- **"Predicting Steering Vectors and Adapter Weights for Few-Shot Author-Style Transfer"** (arXiv 2610.03163, Oct 2026). Qwen3-4B frozen; 3 abstracts per author; 203 seen / 328 unseen authors. Seen-author results (preference rate vs. human text by GPT-OSS-20B judge / kNN style accuracy): zero-shot 97.9% / 31.0%; few-shot 91.5% / 28.1%; **direct LoRA r=8 38.1% / 49.8%**; manual activation steering 94.3% / 34.5%; hypernetwork-predicted LoRA 91.3% / 36.5%. Content-only reference 31.0%, ground-truth ceiling 53.2%. Authors flag that the style metric is content-entangled, English-only, one domain. https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03163
- **Privacy–personalization trade-off** (Arefin & Hammad; arXiv 2609.22112, Aug 2026). LaMP-7 Twitter, 250 users; outputs from full profiles were "nearly indistinguishable" from human text; stripping demographic/cultural/informal cues cut preference to 13.0% while preserving 94.8% semantics — i.e. the *informal* cues are the style. Judged by two LLM judges from different families plus a human check. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22112

### Style representation and evaluation
- **STEL** (Wegmann & Nguyen, EMNLP 2021): modular, content-controlled style-similarity benchmark (formal/informal, simple/complex, contractions, number substitution). BERT-style models beat 3-grams, punctuation frequency and LIWC baselines. Code `nlpsoc/stel`. https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.04817
- **Style Embeddings** (Wegmann, Schraagen, Nguyen, RepL4NLP 2022): `AnnaWegmann/Style-Embedding`, roberta-base, 768-d, trained on `AnnaWegmann/StyleEmbeddingData` (~300k). **No licence field on the model card** (fetched 2026-10-19) — treat as unusable commercially until the author clarifies. https://huggingface.co/AnnaWegmann/Style-Embedding · https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.04907
- **StyleDistance** (arXiv 2410.12757): `StyleDistance/styledistance`, **MIT**, roberta-base (~0.1 B params, BF16), contrastively trained on SynthSTEL (40 style features) + Reddit authorship data; intended for stylistic analysis, clustering, authorship verification and style-transfer evaluation. https://huggingface.co/StyleDistance/styledistance
- **LUAR** (Rivera-Soto et al., EMNLP 2021): `rrivera1849/LUAR-MUD`, **Apache-2.0**, 82.5 M params, outputs one 512-d author embedding per *episode* (a group of texts assumed same-author; episodes in a batch must have equal length). Trained on Reddit Million User Dataset. Variants: LUAR-CRUD; LUAR-MUD-MV-Qwen (Qwen3-0.6B backbone). GitHub LLNL/LUAR Apache-2.0. https://huggingface.co/rrivera1849/LUAR-MUD · https://aclanthology.org/2021.emnlp-main.70
- **LISA — interpretable style embeddings via prompting** (Patel, Rao, Kothary, McKeown, Callison-Burch; arXiv 2305.12696): LLM-annotated stylometry → human-readable style attributes; dataset and models "released as resources"; **licence not stated on the abstract page [unverified]**. https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.12696

### Which evaluation is credible enough to gate on?
- **LLM-as-judge alone: no.** "Can LLM be a Personalized Judge?" (arXiv 2406.11657, Findings EMNLP 2024) finds vanilla LLM-as-personalized-judge has "low and inconsistent agreement with human ground truth"; adding verbal uncertainty and *abstaining* on low-certainty items lifts agreement above 80% on high-certainty binary samples, comparable to third-party humans. https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11657 · https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-emnlp.592.pdf
- Gröner & Chiou (arXiv 2606.16778, Jun 2026): GPT-4o failed to reproduce 30 human participants' behaviour in a writing-style preference task; "hints of bias and a lack of depth in GPT-4o's text generation and judgement". https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16778
- **Automatic style classifiers**: usable as a *gate* only when calibrated against the user's own held-out text (AV framing), because style accuracy is content-entangled (hypernetwork paper) and AI detectors are biased toward GPT-style text (Catch-me paper).
- **Human A/B**: the only ground truth, but NLP human evals are routinely under-powered (Underline talk, "Human evaluations in NLP are even more under-powered than you think"). https://ai.underline.io/lecture/37703-what-happens-if-you-treat-ordinal-ratings-as-interval-dataquestion-human-evaluations-in-nlp-are-even-more-under-powered-than-you-think
- **Conclusion:** gate on (a) calibrated AV score from an open author-embedding model against the user's held-out corpus **and** (b) a blinded pairwise "which of these did you write?" test judged by the user (Echo has a unique advantage: the ground-truth author is the user). Use the uncertainty-aware LLM judge for the in-UI voice-match score, never as the sole gate.

## 2. Prompt-time retrieval vs. fine-tuning

### Evidence
- Few-shot vs. zero-shot: consistent gains, diminishing after ~5 exemplars (Catch-me, §1). Content-matched exemplar retrieval *reduced* attribution — retrieve for **style diversity**, not topical similarity.
- Few-shot vs. LoRA: direct LoRA maximises style signal but destroys quality (38.1% preference) and causes repetition; few-shot keeps quality but adds no style on the abstract task; hypernetwork-LoRA / activation steering sit on the Pareto frontier (arXiv 2610.03163).
- General ICL-vs-FT literature: Mosbach et al. (Findings ACL 2023) — with size controlled, fine-tuning beats ICL at 16 shots. Liu et al. 2022 (T-Few) — few-shot PEFT "better and cheaper than ICL". Many-shot ICL (arXiv 2404.11018) approaches dedicated fine-tuning on some tasks. https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-acl.779/ · https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.05638 · https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.11018
- Training-free critique loops (PerFine, arXiv 2510.24469): +8.5–12% G-Eval over PGraphRAG with 3–5 iterations — see §4.

### Catastrophic forgetting / persona-shift / sycophancy risks
- **Emergent Misalignment** (Betley et al.; arXiv 2502.17424, Feb 2025, v7 Jan 2026): fine-tuning GPT-4o / Qwen2.5-Coder-32B on a narrow task (insecure code) produced broad misalignment on unrelated prompts; the effect could be hidden behind a trigger. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17424
- **Persona Vectors** (Chen, Arditi, Sleight, Evans, Lindsey; arXiv 2507.21509, Jul 2025): intended *and unintended* trait shifts during fine-tuning (evil, **sycophancy**, hallucination) track activation-space directions; these can flag training data likely to cause shifts and support "preventative steering". Directly applicable as a regression test for any Mode B run. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21509
- Direct LoRA style fine-tunes: quality collapse + repetition (arXiv 2610.03163).

## 3. Style-signal extraction techniques

| Technique | Tool | Licence (SPDX) | Size / cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Function-word frequencies, Burrows' Delta | `faststylometry` | MIT | pure Python | Needs tokeniser + reference corpus; calibrate for same-author probability. https://github.com/fastdatascience/faststylometry |
| Readability, dependency distance, POS proportions, coherence, quality, entropy, descriptive stats | `TextDescriptives` | Apache-2.0 | spaCy v3 pipeline; ms per note on CPU | https://github.com/HLasse/TextDescriptives |
| WritePrints feature set + LIWC (Mahalanobis) | as used in Catch-me paper | LIWC commercial | — | https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14543v1 |
| Lexicon categories | **LIWC-22** | proprietary; academic licence restricted to university research; commercial via Receptiviti | — | https://www.liwc.app/buy · https://liwc.app/help/faq |
| Lexicon categories (open) | **Empath** | MIT | ~194–200 categories, r=0.906 with LIWC categories | https://github.com/Ejhfast/empath-client · https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.06979 |
| Emotion lexicons | NRC | research-free, commercial needs permission **[secondary]** | — | https://github.com/dropofwill/py-lex |
| Author embedding | `LUAR-MUD` | Apache-2.0 | 82.5 M params, 512-d, episode input | https://huggingface.co/rrivera1849/LUAR-MUD |
| Style embedding | `StyleDistance` | MIT | ~0.1 B, roberta-base | https://huggingface.co/StyleDistance/styledistance |
| Style embedding | `AnnaWegmann/Style-Embedding` | **none declared** | roberta-base, 768-d | https://huggingface.co/AnnaWegmann/Style-Embedding |
| LLM-extracted interpretable traits | LISA approach; Grammarly trait cards; PerFine critic dims | n/a | one LLM call per batch | https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.12696 |

**Cheap-enough combination for every note:** (1) classical stylometric vector (function-word/punctuation/length/char-n-gram stats — language-agnostic, microseconds), (2) `TextDescriptives` (Apache-2.0) for English, (3) one `LUAR-MUD` or `StyleDistance` embedding per note on the existing CPU sentence-transformers worker (roberta-base-class cost, comparable to the current MiniLM pass). LLM trait extraction runs **batched** (map-reduce like `knowledge_extract_batch`) and on demand, not per note. All three open embeddings are trained on English Reddit; see §7 for the Hindi/Hinglish gap.

## 4. Agentic draft–critique loops

- **Self-Refine** (Madaan et al. 2023; arXiv 2303.17651) is the origin: one model generates, critiques and refines with no extra training. https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17651
- **PerFine — Iterative Critique-Refine for personalization** (arXiv 2510.24469, Oct 2025): generator conditioned on a retrieved user profile; a critic conditioned on the *same* profile returns structured feedback on **tone, vocabulary, sentence structure, topicality**; a "knockout" keeps the stronger draft across iterations. Reported: +8.5–12% G-Eval over PGraphRAG on Yelp/Goodreads/Amazon; steady improvement over 3–5 iterations; gains scale with critic size; **no human evaluation**. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24469
- Adjacent evidence that self-critique moves faithfulness-type properties: arXiv 2505.22823 cut unfaithful explanations from ~55% to 36%. Counter-evidence: intrinsic self-correction results are mixed (Valmeekam et al. on planning; arXiv 2406.02378). https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22823
- **Critique vs. more examples:** no head-to-head paper found. Catch-me shows exemplar count saturates at ~5; PerFine shows iteration gains — different setups, so the honest reading is "cheap exemplars first, then 2–3 critique rounds, and measure".
- **"Constitutional" style checks:** no literature specific to style was found this session. The closest working pattern is PerFine's profile-conditioned rubric; a per-user "style constitution" (trait card + prohibited moves) used as the critic rubric is a reasonable design, but unvalidated.
- **Deviation notes in products:** Grammarly exposes the inferred trait list and lets the user discard traits (TechCrunch 2023). The hypernetwork paper's *preference-rate* metric is a useful "quality guard" to report alongside the style score so a draft that matches style but reads badly is flagged.

## 6. Safety and abuse

- **OpenAI Usage Policies** (effective 2025-10-29): prohibit "deceit, fraud, scams, spam, or impersonation" and "use of someone's likeness, including their photorealistic image or voice, without their consent in ways that could confuse authenticity" **[quoted via search summary; direct fetch returned 403]**. ChatGPT-agent guidance: "Impersonating individuals or organizations without consent or legal right". Enforcement precedent: Dean.bot account removal, Jan 2024. https://openai.com/policies/usage-policies/ · https://openai.com/policies/using-chatgpt-agent-in-line-with-our-policies/ · https://www.theregister.com/2024/01/22/openai_presidential_candidate_bot/
- **EU AI Act Article 50** (https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/transparency-rules-article-50/ · https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/transparency-obligations-under-article-50-ai-act):
  - 50(1) systems that interact directly with people must disclose AI (a floating chat bubble is in scope); 50(2) providers must mark synthetic text/audio/image/video machine-readably; 50(4) deployers must disclose deepfakes and AI-generated text *published to inform the public on matters of public interest*, with an exception where the text "has undergone human review or editorial control" and a person holds editorial responsibility.
  - Dates: applicable **2026-08-02**; AI Omnibus (May 2026 provisional agreement) defers 50(2) marking to **2026-12-02** for generative systems already on the market; watermark-detection interoperability deferred to 2027-02-02; Commission Guidelines finalised 2026-07-20.
  - Code of Practice on transparency of AI-generated content (final plenary 2026-06-10; ~190 signatories by 2026-07-31; voluntary): ≥2 machine-readable marking techniques (e.g. signed metadata + imperceptible watermark); **one technique suffices for free-form text that cannot carry metadata; text under ~200 tokens (~150 words) exempt from watermarking**; assistive editing (grammar/spell check, format conversion) and short outputs (captions, labels) are outside 50(2). Three EU icons: fully AI-generated, AI-modified, basic + interactive layer. https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/code-practice-ai-generated-content · https://www.paulweiss.com/insights/client-memos/eu-finalises-transparency-rules-for-ai-generated-content
  - Private email/message drafts: not within 50(4) (a reply seen only by its requester is explicitly not covered); 50(1) still applies to the conversational bubble.
- **India**: IT Rules amendment **G.S.R. 120(E), notified 2026-02-10** (effective 2026-02-20 per iPleaders; Legal500 says "effective immediately" with a 10-day deployment deadline — sources conflict). "Synthetically generated information" = AI-created or substantially altered **audio, visual or audiovisual** content that appears authentic; minor edits and conceptual documents excluded; **AI-generated text is not covered**. Obligations: prominent labels, tamper-proof metadata/identifiers, user self-declaration on upload, automated verification by significant intermediaries, quarterly reminders; takedown windows reported as 2–3 h (government orders) and 15 min for deepfake grievances **[figures conflict across sources — check the gazette]**. Draft was published 2025-10-22. https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/india/media-telecoms-it-entertainment/2026-it-rules-amendments-ai-content-labelling-and-intermediary-obligations · https://blog.ipleaders.in/it-rules-2026/ · https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/10/8e40cdd134cd92dd783a37556428c370.pdf
- **Rate limits in comparable products**: Grammarly Free 100 AI prompts/month, Pro 2,000/member/month, Enterprise unlimited **[third-party pricing pages, Jul–Sep 2026]**; NotebookLM compute-based limits with 5-hour refresh (official). Notion AI / Gmail "Help me write" limits not verified. https://costbench.com/software/ai-writing-tools/grammarly/
- **Disclosure a drafted message should carry** (synthesis): (a) a visible, user-removable line such as "Drafted with Echo, reviewed by <user>" — defaults on; the "reviewed by" clause is what the 50(4) editorial-responsibility exception keys on if the text is ever published; (b) machine-readable marking in any export (metadata where the container supports it; for pasted free text only one technique is feasible under the Code); (c) the bubble itself labelled as AI per 50(1); (d) never auto-send (already in spec); (e) provenance guard: only the tenant's own authored material may feed a voice, so third-party impersonation is structurally impossible.

## 7. Multilingual voice

- **Bilingual personality shift**: Ramírez-Esparza et al. 2006 ("Do bilinguals have two personalities? A special case of cultural frame switching") — Spanish–English bilinguals scored higher on extraversion, agreeableness and conscientiousness when tested in English; mechanism framed as cultural frame switching. A Chinese–English replication found near-identical ratings, so the effect is real but modest and language-pair dependent. https://findanexpert.unimelb.edu.au/scholarlywork/1207916-do-bilinguals-have-two-personalities%3F-a-special-case-of-cultural-frame-switching · https://www.psychologytoday.com/au/blog/life-bilingual/202001/change-language-change-personality-uncovered-data
- **Hindi–English register** (Rudra, Rijhwani, Begum, Bali, Choudhury, Ganguly; EMNLP 2016, 430,000 tweets; text extracted from the PDF): "Hindi (i.e., the native language) is preferred over English for expression of negative opinion and swearing"; odds of negative-over-positive in Hindi 1.5–6 vs 0.1–0.6 in English; within code-switched tweets the Hindi negative/positive odds are **6.5×** English's; a switch English→Hindi correlates with positive→negative; "English is more preferred for expressing positive opinion"; swearing strongly prefers Hindi. The paper also cites Fishman (1971): English for professional purposes, L1 for informal chat. https://aclanthology.org/D16-1121/
- Bali, Sharma, Choudhury, Vyas (CodeSwitch 2014): English-Hindi Facebook data — borrowing vs. mixing forms a continuum; mixed text carries non-standard spelling, transliteration and grammar, which breaks monolingual tooling. https://aclanthology.org/W14-3914/
- Survey of code-switched NLP in the LLM era (arXiv 2510.07037). https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.07037
- **Implications for the signal schema**: every voice signal needs `language` (en / hi-Deva / hi-Latn Hinglish / mixed) and `register` (intimate, casual, professional, public) dimensions; style statistics and exemplar retrieval must be bucketed per language so an English professional email is not drafted from Hinglish WhatsApp exemplars. The existing script-based `language_service` cannot see romanised Hinglish (documented gap in CLAUDE.md), so signal tagging needs a token-level LID for Latin-script Hindi. LUAR/StyleDistance/Wegmann are all English-Reddit-trained — for Hindi/Hinglish fall back to language-agnostic stylometry (char n-grams, punctuation, length, per-language function-word lists) and treat embedding-based voice-match as English-only until a multilingual style model is validated.

## 9. Dataset export formats for fine-tuning (2026)

- **OpenAI chat JSONL** — still the format on the SFT guide: one JSON object per line, `{"messages":[{"role":"user"|"assistant"|…,"content":…}]}`, optional `tools`, `tool_calls`, `parallel_tool_calls`; min 10 lines; the archived cookbook validator additionally accepts `system`, `name`, `function_call`, `weight`, enforces ≥1 assistant turn, flags >16,385 tokens (cl100k_base) and estimates epochs (target 3, min 100 / max 25,000 examples). The platform is closing (§2), but this remains the de-facto interchange format. https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/supervised-fine-tuning · https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/chat_finetuning_data_prep
- **Bedrock / Anthropic Claude 3 Haiku** — `{"system": "...", "messages":[{"role":"user","content":…},{"role":"assistant","content":…}]}`, single- or multi-turn; 32–10,000 training lines, 32–1,000 validation, <32k tokens per entry, avoid literal `\nHuman:` / `\nAssistant:`; 10 GB / 1 GB file caps. Nova uses a different schema **[secondary]**. https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/fine-tuning-for-anthropics-claude-3-haiku-model-in-amazon-bedrock-is-now-generally-available/
- **Vertex AI Gemini** — `{"systemInstruction":{"parts":[{"text":…}]}, "contents":[{"role":"user"|"model","parts":[{"text":…}|{"fileData":{"mimeType","fileUri"}}]}], "tools":[…]}`; uploaded to Cloud Storage; ~100 examples to start; validation set strongly recommended. https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/models/gemini-supervised-tuning-prepare
- **Hugging Face TRL (v1.16 docs)** — conversational *language-modeling* `{"messages":[…]}` or *prompt-completion* `{"prompt":[…],"completion":[…]}` for `SFTTrainer`; preference `{"prompt","chosen","rejected"}` for DPO/ORPO/CPO; unpaired `{"prompt","completion","label"}` for KTO; `tools` column for tool-calling; Harmony format (developer role, analysis/final channels) for gpt-oss; chat template auto-applied; `assistant_only_loss=True` needs `{% generation %}` support in the template; conversion helpers `extract_prompt`, `unpair_preference_dataset`, plus `examples/datasets/*` scripts. https://huggingface.co/docs/trl/en/dataset_formats · https://huggingface.co/docs/trl/main/en/sft_trainer
- **Validation tooling**: OpenAI's notebook (archived, gpt-3.5-era limits) and TRL's converters are the only maintained public pieces; a project-specific validator (JSON-schema per target, role alternation, token counts via `tiktoken` / `tokenizer.apply_chat_template`, dedupe, PII scrub, per-language bucket counts) is the practical answer.
- **Implication for Pratidhvani**: keep the existing OpenAI-chat JSONL as the canonical interchange (TRL's `messages` format is byte-compatible), add thin exporters for Bedrock-Claude (`system` + `messages`), Vertex (`contents`/`parts`) and TRL prompt-completion, and tag every example with `language`, `register`, `source_id`, `consent_version` so themes can be curated and revoked.

## Recommendations

**Build first (low cost, no new vendors):**
1. **Voice-signal store** keyed by `(tenant_id, signal_type, language, register)` with provenance (`source_id`, char offsets), `consent_version`, and revocation that cascades to any derived trait and to the Q&A/knowledge Chroma rows. Signals are rows, not prompt blobs — this is what makes them inspectable/editable/revocable.
2. **Style layer on the existing CPU worker**: per-note language-agnostic stylometry (function-word/punctuation/length/char-n-gram stats; Burrows' Delta via `faststylometry`, MIT) + `TextDescriptives` (Apache-2.0) for English + one `LUAR-MUD` (Apache-2.0) *or* `StyleDistance` (MIT) embedding. Skip LIWC-22 (commercial) and Wegmann's model (no licence).
3. **Personality layer by batched LLM extraction** reusing the knowledge-agent map-reduce: emit `{trusted_conclusions, preferred_solutions, recommendation_lens, apprehensions, methodology, topic_emphasis}` as citations-backed JSON per batch, merge with dedupe, render an editable trait card (Grammarly-style "discard this trait").
4. **Speak-as-me v1 = retrieval + critique, not fine-tuning**: retrieve ~5 exemplars chosen for *style diversity within the target language/register* (not topical similarity — it hurt attribution), inject the trait card, draft, then run a PerFine-style critic (tone / vocabulary / sentence structure / topicality / "moves the user never makes") with knockout for 2–3 rounds; return draft + deviation notes + citations.
5. **Voice-match score** = calibrated AV cosine (LUAR/StyleDistance) against the user's held-out per-language centroid, shown with a confidence band; LLM judge only with abstention; never the sole gate.
6. **Readiness gate** = count floor per language bucket (suggest ≥30 notes or ≥2,000 words, numbers to be tuned) **and** stability (bootstrap variance of the style centroid below ε) **and** a passed blinded "which did you write?" session (user cannot beat chance over N=10 pairs). Show a per-language progress meter in the bubble.
7. **Dataset exporters** for Bedrock-Claude, Vertex and TRL formats now (cheap), so Mode B data is ready whichever provider survives.

**Defer:**
- **Mode B fine-tuning** until the hosted picture settles: OpenAI is closing; Claude is Haiku-3-only on Bedrock with Provisioned Throughput; Gemini 2.5 is retiring and 3.x pricing is unverified. If pursued, do it **locally** on an open-weights 8B (Unsloth core / PEFT, Apache-2.0; 6 GB VRAM floor, 12–16 GB realistic), with a *preference-rate quality guard* and persona-vector-style regression prompts, because direct LoRA style tuning is documented to collapse quality.
- Multilingual style embeddings and Hinglish token-level LID — needed before the voice-match score is trusted outside English.
- Constitutional-style critics and hypernetwork/steering approaches — promising but unvalidated.

**Evaluation to adopt:** three tiers — (i) automatic AV gate, (ii) uncertainty-aware LLM judge for the UI score, (iii) periodic blinded user-as-judge pairwise test as the product gate — plus quality guard (preference vs. a neutral draft) so style wins never ship unreadable text.

## Risks and open questions

- **Vendor risk**: OpenAI fine-tuning shutdown (2027-01-06 / 2027-01-14); Gemini 2.5 retirement (Oct 2026, exact date conflicting); Claude fine-tuning frozen at Haiku 3 in one region. Any Mode B roadmap must be provider-agnostic from day one.
- **Measurement risk**: every automatic style metric is content-entangled; AI detectors are biased; LLM judges are shallow unless they abstain; human evals are under-powered. The user-as-judge test is the credible anchor, and it costs user time.
- **Multilingual gap**: no open style embedding for Hindi/Hinglish; script-based LID misses romanised Hindi; register shifts per language are real (Rudra 2016) so single-language signals will mis-voice bilingual users.
- **Fine-tuning safety**: narrow fine-tunes can shift broad traits (emergent misalignment) and sycophancy (persona vectors); direct LoRA on style degrades quality. Needs regression prompts and a quality guard before any adapter is served.
- **Licensing**: LIWC-22 commercial; NRC needs permission for commercial use; Wegmann Style-Embedding has no licence; Unsloth Studio UI is AGPL-3.0 (core is Apache-2.0); Llama 3.x is under Meta's community licence.
- **Regulatory**: EU 50(1) applies to the bubble from 2026-08-02; 50(2) free-text marking is weakly defined (single technique, <200-token exemption) and watermarking pasted text is impractical — rely on visible disclosure + metadata in exports; India currently excludes text but the rules changed within four months of the draft; OpenAI/Anthropic/Google usage policies all bar impersonating *others* — the provenance guard (tenant-authored sources only) must be enforced, not advisory.
- **Unverified in this session** (search budget exhausted / pages blocked): Microsoft Copilot "Sound like me" launch and threshold; Vertex per-token tuning prices; Grammarly's 200-word minimum; Delphi/Personal.ai/Character.AI/Replika data minimums; Apple personal-context Siri ship status; exact current OpenAI usage-policy wording (403); Intercom accessibility documentation; Notion AI shortcut; Bedrock Claude customization prices.
