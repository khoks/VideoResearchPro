Connect to your Pratidhvani

On Pratidhvani's Echo page, under "Add from your browser", make a share token and paste it here with the server address shown beside it. The token can only add to your Echo inbox; it cannot read anything.

The extension sends something only when you pick "Add … to Echo" from the right-click menu or the toolbar button. It has no access to your browsing history. Quotes are kept as someone else's words unless you mark them as yours in the Echo inbox.
